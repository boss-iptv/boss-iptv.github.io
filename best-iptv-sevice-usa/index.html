What Is an IPTV Service?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers live TV channels and on-demand video through your internet connection — not a satellite dish or cable line. You can watch on Smart TVs, Firestick, Android boxes, smartphones, tablets, and PCs.

A good IPTV subscription in 2026 typically includes 5,000–20,000+ live channels, a VOD library, catch-up TV, and multi-screen support. Prices range from budget monthly plans to long-term subscriptions that work out as low as $4–$6/month.
